slack-tui▏

A keyboard-first Slack client for your terminal.

Like vim or lazygit — not another Electron window.

  • Vim-modal — j/k, gg/G, / to find, dd to delete
  • Threads, reactions, edits — and fuzzy search over everything
  • Lives in the background — live unread, notifications on mentions
  • Follows your desktop theme — on Omarchy
  • Your own Slack app — no third party sees your messages
